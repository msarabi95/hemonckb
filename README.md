# HemOnc KB

HemOnc KB is my personal knowledge base for hematology and oncology, built on **TiddlyWiki5**. I use it to bring together brief, succinct, and practical notes for quick reference in patient care and focused board review.

## Scope

I organize my notes around three areas:

- **Classical hematology:** nonmalignant blood disorders, including anemia, bleeding, and thrombosis.
- **Malignant hematology:** hematologic malignancies, including leukemias, lymphomas, and plasma cell disorders.
- **Solid oncology:** solid tumors, their evaluation, and approaches to treatment.

## Approach

My goal is to capture the essentials: key clinical features, diagnostic frameworks, treatment principles, and high-yield distinctions. I aim to keep my notes clear and practical, with enough context to support understanding and recall.

I intend to grow and refine HemOnc KB over time as a personal reference for clinical learning and review.

## Repository

I use TiddlyWiki5 to organize individual notes, called *tiddlers*, into an interconnected wiki.

- `tiddlers/` contains the wiki's notes and configuration tiddlers.
- `tiddlywiki.info` defines the wiki's plugins, themes, and build settings.
